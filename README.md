# The CS Plan

The CS Plan aims to become the academic planning and tracking companion for NUS Computing students. We hope to help students plan modules across their entire duration of study, and see how the current plan contributes to their degree requirements, making planning decisions with less manual cross-checking. We also hope to help students track their pre/post S/U GPA, S/U usage, and simulate what grades are required to hit a target GPA.

Our goal is convenience and clarity in academic planning and tracking for NUS students.
